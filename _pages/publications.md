My publications focus on glacier-ocean interactions, Greenland fjords, polar ocean modeling, ocean observations, bathymetry, and machine learning applications in cryosphere and marine science. I am grateful for the collaborations represented in these papers and for the many colleagues, students, mentors, and project teams I have learned from along the way.
